FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Ping Master Pro.

Is Ping Master Pro really free?
+

Yes! The core speed testing, ping analysis, and network diagnostics are 100% free. We offer an optional Pro upgrade for advanced features like unlimited history and custom alerts.

How accurate are the speed tests?
+

Ping Master Pro uses multi-threaded testing with optimized packet sizes to match results from Ookla, Fast.com, and major ISPs within 1-2% accuracy. Our algorithm is calibrated against reference servers worldwide.

Does it work on all Android devices?
+

Ping Master Pro supports Android 6.0 (Marshmallow) and above. It's optimized for both phones and tablets, and works seamlessly on WiFi, 4G, 5G, and LTE connections.

Is my data private and secure?
+

Absolutely. All test data is stored locally on your device by default. We never sell user data, and you can export or delete your history anytime. Privacy is our top priority.

Can I use it to diagnose slow internet?
+

Yes! Ping Master Pro includes detailed diagnostics that help identify whether the issue is with your ISP, router, WiFi signal, or device. The historical comparison feature helps track performance over time.
